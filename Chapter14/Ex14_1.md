### Exercise 14.1

An object has an inertia tensor of

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
a & 0 & 0 \\
0 & b & 0 \\
0 & 0 & c
\end{bmatrix}
$$

What is its inverse inertia tensor? If you know how to invert a matrix algebraically, you can do that to solve this problem. Alternatively, you can either work through the matrix inversion code by hand, or experiment with its results until you see the pattern (it shouldn’t be difficult to spot).

### Solution

$$
\begin{bmatrix}
1/a & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 1/b & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1/c
\end{bmatrix}
$$
